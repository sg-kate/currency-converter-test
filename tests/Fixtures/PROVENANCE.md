## Why error-429.json is not captured live

Provoking a real 429 means exhausting the per-minute limit or the entire monthly
quota. Neither is an acceptable price for a fixture, so the body is written from the
documented shape. It is a single field — `{"message":"Too Many Requests"}` — and the
part that actually matters for the client is the **headers**, not the body: the body
does not say whether the minute or the month was exhausted, and the tests supply those
headers directly.
